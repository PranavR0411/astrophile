About

I’m a master’s student in physics at Imperial College London, working at the intersection of observation and computation. I use data from the James Webb Space Telescope and large surveys such as COSMOS, and build tools to interpret how stars form, how dust and gas interact, and how galaxies assemble and change over billions of years.

I have worked with data across the electromagnetic spectrum, from instruments including the VLA, Fermi-LAT, AstroSat/UVIT and JWST. Studying the same systems in different bands shows how star formation, feedback and dust emission appear at different energies. Integrating those datasets with statistical and physical models is at the heart of my work.

When I’m not studying galaxies, you’ll find me hiking, or with a book and a cup of coffee.
