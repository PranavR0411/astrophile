Projects

Research, coursework and competition projects, with the methods and results of each.

The N-body simulation and the review of Jupiter’s moons are not tied to a single band, so they appear in the lists only.

Research

  • A photometric population model for Lyman break galaxies

    MSc thesis / Jul 2025 – Jun 2026

    Building a data-driven, SED-based population model for galaxies at z > 4, where current models such as pop-cosmos lose coverage.

  • Spatial analysis of PAHs in the Pillars of Creation

    Independent research / Jul 2025 – Oct 2025

    Mapping PAH size and ionisation across the Pillars of Creation with JWST, and tracing how radiation from NGC 6611 shapes them.

  • A search for radio transients in the VLA COSMOS field

    Research internship / Jul 2025 – Oct 2025

    Calibrating, imaging and mosaicing 7,040 VLA images of the COSMOS field to search for sources that vary in brightness.

  • Far-ultraviolet emission from novae in the Magellanic Clouds

    Research internship / Jun 2024 – Aug 2024

    Identifying optical novae in archival AstroSat/UVIT images of the SMC and LMC and measuring their far-UV fluxes.

  • Gamma-ray variability of neutrino-candidate blazars

    Research project / Jul 2023 – Jul 2024

    Building optimally binned Fermi-LAT light curves to test whether blazars associated with IceCube neutrinos flare differently.

Coursework and competitions

  • Exospheres and ionospheres of Jupiter’s icy moons

    Course project (literature review) / Jan 2025 – Mar 2025

    A synthesis of about 100 papers comparing how Europa, Ganymede and Callisto build and sustain their thin atmospheres.

  • An N-body galaxy and the case for dark matter

    Course project / Mar 2025

    Simulating a rotating galaxy from first principles to show that flat rotation curves need a dark matter halo.

  • Unsupervised clustering of the Hipparcos H–R diagram

    Course project / Aug 2022 – Dec 2022

    Recovering stellar populations from 118,218 Hipparcos stars with DBSCAN, validated against derived temperatures and radii.

  • Detecting and classifying solar X-ray bursts

    Inter IIT Tech Meet (team of five) / Feb 2022 – Mar 2022

    A peak-detection and clustering pipeline for noisy Chandrayaan-2 solar X-ray light curves, built for an ISRO challenge.
